# The $1B Decision — 1TCC × BCG

An interactive CFO simulation game built for the BCG exhibition booth.

🎮 **[Play the game](https://1tccdev.github.io/tcc-game/)**

---

## About

You are CFO of a major manufacturer. Make 5 supply chain decisions under time pressure. Every choice moves your balance sheet — live.

Built by **1TCC** in partnership with **BCG**.

---

## Features

- 📊 Live FCF bar chart with dollar labels — your result vs 1TCC optimal
- 🏢 550 US & European investment grade companies (ticker dropdown auto-fill)
- ✅ Decision-by-decision breakdown on results screen
- 📈 FCF & Market Cap comparison: your result vs 1TCC optimal (5/5)
- 📩 Microsoft Forms lead capture
- 📋 Game start logging to Google Sheets
- 📱 Mobile friendly
- 🔒 GDPR-compliant opt-in consent
